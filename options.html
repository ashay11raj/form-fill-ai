FormFill AI settings

Everything is stored in this browser. Your profile is sent only to the AI provider you choose below, and only when you fill a form.

AI provider

A custom URL asks Chrome for permission to reach that host when you save.

Profiles

The agent answers only from the active profile. Keep one per person or purpose, for example Personal, Work, or a family member. Add anything forms tend to ask: addresses, contacts, links, canned answers under custom_answers.

Resume file (optional)

Attached automatically to resume/CV upload fields. A PDF also lets Claude build a profile for you.

Preferences

Off by default. When on, ID numbers in your profile are sent to your AI provider. Card details, passwords and one-time codes are never filled either way.
